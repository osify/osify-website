WOT site rated the website [newsdaily7.com](http://www.mywot.com/en/scorecard/newsdaily7.com) is a lowest reputation.

But another way of this site made by spreading the advertising through some popular site such as Yahoo as in my colleague’s case.

[![newsdaily7.com scams on Yahoo home page via Softonic-Eng7 Community Toolbar](/media/dc/dc717efbb238-newsdaily7-com-fake-1.webp "newsdaily7.com-fake-1")](/media/dc/dc717efbb238-newsdaily7-com-fake-1.webp)

newsdaily7.com scams on Yahoo home page via Softonic-Eng7 Community Toolbar

If you saw above home page of Yahoo with ads as: **Google Pays Me $128 an Hour** and link the ads to newsdaily7.com, it’s a scam and consider to check your firefox add-on and search to find:

- Add-on: Softonic-Eng7 Community Toolbar
- Version: 3.5.0.12 (or different)
- Toolbar created from website:  conduit.com (So it can be in different company)

[![newsdaily7.com scams on Yahoo home page via Softonic-Eng7 Community Toolbar](/media/09/09c1566ba867-newsdaily7-com-fake-2-addon-softonic.webp "newsdaily7.com-fake-2-addon-softonic")](/media/09/09c1566ba867-newsdaily7-com-fake-2-addon-softonic.webp)

newsdaily7.com scams on Yahoo home page via Softonic-Eng7 Community Toolbar

Stay safe from those add-on, reduce your add-on installs if they are not important or if you are not clear or from the unknown company.
