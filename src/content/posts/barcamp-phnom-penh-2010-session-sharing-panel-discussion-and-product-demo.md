Barcamp Phnom Penh 3 2010

BarCamp Phnom Penh is an international, innovative, and open technology conference. It’s for individuals, businesses, and non-profits. [This two-day gathering](http://barcampphnompenh.org/venue/) at University of Puthisastra (UP) is aimed at computer enthusiasts, Internet users, technologists, entrepreneurs, digital journalists, web developers and netizens to share and learn all things technology in a very open and democratic environment.

**What is going to have?**

At the event, you will see many sessions sharing, product demos and panel discussion all related to technologies, social medias and business concept via technology.

**Who could attend?**

Everyone, especially for those who willing to gain more knowledge in Information Technology (IT) ~ to know how to use the technology for your business.

- Good for all experts in various computer skill to share, to show up themselves by make a presentation
- Good for all IT engineers, technicians to gain more knowledge
- Good for business people to find partnership among experts who present the topic
- Good for students to go to learn quick concept and technique

**When, Where, How much?**

- On Saturday & Sunday of 25th – 26th of September, from 8am to 5pm
- At University of Puthisastra (#55, St.180, Sangkat Boeung Raing, Khan Daun Penh, Phnom Penh)
- Cost is free for everyone ~ including some free stuffs given by our sponsor

**More information**

Please register and check at website: [http://barcampphnompenh.org](http://barcampphnompenh.org/)
