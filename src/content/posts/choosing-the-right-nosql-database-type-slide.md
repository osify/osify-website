NoSQL is the next big thing in big data. Since the technology is relatively new undergoing innovations every now and then, it is safe to say there are no defined standards related to NoSQL database.

NoSQL databases most popularly get categorized into the following four types:

- Key-Value Store
- Document-based Store
- Column-based Store
- Graph-based
