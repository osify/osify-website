Now we have Khmer language translation available in [Google translator](http://translate.google.com/#auto/en/%E1%9E%9F%E1%9E%BD%E1%9E%9F%E1%9F%92%E1%9E%8F%E1%9E%B8!%20%E1%9E%A2%E1%9E%9A%E1%9E%82%E1%9E%BB%E1%9E%8E%E1%9E%85%E1%9F%92%E1%9E%9A%E1%9E%BE%E1%9E%93) that is really a new big step forward to let the world understand Cambodia through our language.

You can now translate from Khmer to any language and vice versa.

To translate from Khmer to other language, you need to write using Khmer unicode.

Thank Google and [thank to developer](http://osify.com/2013/03/google-translate-may-include-khmer-this-year/ "Google Translate may include Khmer this year!").
