First reading the word “Hacker”, some of you feel afraid to continue reading for non-tech persons but will keep continuing reading the article for tech-person as myself.

Through Wikipedia explains:  [Hackerspaces](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackerspace) are community-operated physical places, where people can meet and work on their projects.

So it’s where all people meet each other for any kind of business and that would be great for freelancers or even other guru to join the project.

I’m not an expert on it so let’s go to see new hacker spaces in Phnom Penh, visit [here](http://www.hackerspacepp.org/):

> Building the space is only half the story, without interesting members and fun or innovative project it will never reach it’s full potential. So the next challenge is to get the geek community of Phnom Penh and beyond interested in using the facilities and up to speed on what can be expected from a world class HackerSpace!

Kind of fun and new to Cambodia!

Contact them, you gonna have free membership for this new year of 2011 as well:

> The free membership will run from the time you sign up until February 1st 2011.
> Detail [read here](http://www.hackerspacepp.org/2010/12/30/hackerspacepp-beta-free-membership-for-one-month/).

Welcome #HackerSpacePP!
