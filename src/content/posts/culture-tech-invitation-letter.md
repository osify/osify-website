Just my first surprise receiving one of the creative wedding invitation letter this year which is mixed between culture and tech idea.

That’s a wedding invitation from my friend, a tech guy in Cambodia.

The cover is using the flute’s box from Siem Reap, an Angkor Wat residence and using the paper decorates as some leafs with both color and feel. And there is a QR code of the party’s date as well.

It looks like a letter in our ancient time, It’s nice and very creative idea as a tech person.

That’s a great memory for them. Wish them a happy married life.
