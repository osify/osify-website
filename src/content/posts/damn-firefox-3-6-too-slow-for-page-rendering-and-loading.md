Too bad for firefox 3.6 that’s too slow for loading or rendering page for any website.

I’m a fan of firefox for years but this time really make me sad to see a big opensource browser starting to fail, most of my development I use firefox as a core testing and recommendation browser to my clients and users and now may be it’s time to change.

Tested with the same sites to load in firefox and google chrom found Google chrom wins to firefox.

I don’t like also IE and now maybe it’s time for firefox but I do hope it resolves very soon as I don’t want to change much.

See all mentions about this issue at mozilla forum as well:

<https://support.mozilla.com/en-US/forum/1/584179#threadId647700>

My firefox has almost no extension (I have few, but the same issue when I disable or uninstall)

Any recommendation to optimize for firefox 3.6 is welcome.
