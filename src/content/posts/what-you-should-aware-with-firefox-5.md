### Fabulous Tabs

That’s what it featured in their “what’s new” page of firefox 5, so don’t forget to have a look to those features:

- **App Tabs**: Make a permanent home for the sites you visit most. [Learn more.](http://support.mozilla.com/en-US/kb/what-are-app-tabs)
- **Switch to Tab**: Find already open tabs using the Awesome Bar. [Learn more.](http://support.mozilla.com/en-US/kb/Location%20bar%20autocomplete?s=awesome+bar&as=s#w_what-is-switch-to-tab)
- **Panorama**: Organize your open tabs into groups. [Learn more.](http://support.mozilla.com/en-US/kb/what-are-tab-groups)
