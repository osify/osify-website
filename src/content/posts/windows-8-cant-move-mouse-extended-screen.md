You use extended screen on Windows 8 or 8.1 but you can’t move your mouse from one screen to another screen.

It’s the same issue raised on [SuperUser](http://superuser.com/questions/688762/unable-to-move-my-mouse-to-second-screen-windows-8) site but seem the solution unclear for me.

Myself could solve it with changing the graphic option of *Output (To)* the extended desktop with *Digital Display + Built-in Display* as shown below:
