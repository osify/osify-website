You might see some messy jar in view of your eclipse project in Project Explorer and you want to remove it from this view. Here is a short instruction for that.

## Instructions

1. On Eclipse Project Explorer, your left top menu of the explorer, click on **Filter**
2. On the “Java Element Filters”, find “Libraries from external”
   1. Tick it in order to disable view
   2. Disable tick it in order to show all external jars
