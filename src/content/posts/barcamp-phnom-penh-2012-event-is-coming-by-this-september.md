Barcamp Phnom Penh 2012, 22-23 September

BarCamp Phnom Penh is an international, innovative, and open technology conference. It’s for individuals, businesses, and non-profits who are computer enthusiasts, Internet users, technologists, entrepreneurs, digital journalists, web developers and netizens to share and learn all things technology in a very open and democratic environment.
