If you want to activate twitter text messaging with mobile in Cambodia, you can now use with any mobile operators.

Just go to your twitter settings, click on Mobile, input your phone number and select your carrier:

- Beeline
- CellCard
- Excell
- Hello
- MetFone
- MFone (*)
- qb
- Smart Mobile

Let’s activate now.

(*) might not work with very soon since it’s integrating with “CellCard” now.
