Cambodia with a growing of economics by each year, technologies also rise faster now among all people through mobile phone and Internet.

Many business, individual starts love to have their own business mobile app as well. That’s why there are many growing companies here.

Here, I found two interesting apps to introduce about finding location in Cambodia for daily use and for tourism.

## 5mn M-tourism

The application is a unique one focusing on tourism on Cambodia. The application is developed by [Nokor GROUP](http://nokor-group.com), an IT company.

![5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover](/media/9d/9d665fb4cd56-5mn-find-cambodia-1.webp "5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover")

5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover

![5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover](/media/76/76d69256023a-5mn-find-cambodia-2.webp "5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover")

5mn Tourism on iTune Store Cover

This mobile app is native app, created to help a tourist visiting in Cambodia to find quickly the information from his Smartphone:

- Cambodia Accommodation
- Activities, Events & Transport in Cambodia
- Interactive Maps
- Healthcare Information.
- Location Based Services
- Restaurants
- Direct from here map (Road suggestion with the nearest on the map)
- Smart place suggestion with user profile
- Cost saving – Online/Offline data display (Every data will be updated from server one time only and store in the local device)

This app has categorized each location in different categories and user can quickly response to each location by direct call or review each location by comment.

You can find for download for **FREE**:

- Cambodia, iTune Store: <https://itunes.apple.com/kh/app/5mn-m-tourism/id592310666?ls=1&mt=8>
  - App Release on 14 January 2013
  - App Size: 3.9MB
  - Work both Offline and online (to synchronize when data update)
  - Language: English and Khmer (Unicode)
  - Version 1.0
- Google Play Store: [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nokor.fiveminutes](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nokor.fiveminutes "Five Minutes - Tourism")
  - App Release on 31 January 2013
  - App Size: 1.3MB
  - Work both Offline and online
  - Language English and Khmer (Unicode)
  - Version 1.0

## Find Khmer

Find Khmer is a Cambodian local search engine based on location awareness. The app uses a database from [FindKH](http://www.findkh.com).

![Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover](/media/9d/9de748162fa6-find-cambodia-1.webp "Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover")

Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover

![Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover](/media/2b/2b22313fb310-find-cambodia-2.webp "Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover")

Find Cambodia on iTune Store Cover

Features:

- Online search by categories, locations
- Search the nearest points of interest
- Phone call, Send email and view Map (phone call available on iPhone only)
- Option to choose the distant for “Near Me” function

This app is categorized by different location and type of services which you can browse it by My Category, My Location, Near Me in Android (I haven’t checked for Apple yet).

You can download for **FREE**:

- Cambodia, iTune Store: <https://itunes.apple.com/kh/app/find-khmer/id520634066?mt=8>
  - Last Update: 13 December 2012
  - App Size: 40.2 MB
  - Work online only (require Internet connection)
  - Language: English / Khmer
  - Version 2.0
- Google Play Store: <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.find.khmer>
  - Last update: November 5, 2012
  - App Size: 2.9 MB
  - Version 1.1

Both of apps are useful.
