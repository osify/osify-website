Suggestion from Google

> For the best Gmail performance, we suggest disabling Firebug for www.google.com.
>
> You can find instructions for the most recent version of Firebug at the [Firebug documentation site](http://getfirebug.com/wiki/index.php/Main_Page).
>
> If disabling Firebug for Gmail doesn’t improve performance results, you may have to entirely disable Firebug.
>
> <http://mail.google.com/support/bin/answer.py?hl=en&ctx=mail&answer=77355>
