Barcamp Phnom Penh is tech. event happened every year since 2008 to gather most of students and professionals locally in Cambodia. It’s also a big session sharing event in Cambodia that broadcasts the state of the art of the way of sharing through presentations and talks.

This year, 2011, it will happen on 22nd – 23rd October 2011 at University of Puthisastra, Phnom Penh, CAMBODIA.

Let’s see what’s new on Barcamp Phnom Penh 2011?

# Venue

Barcamp Phnom Penh this year got a well welcome from University of Puthisastra same place as last year but we have new halls to host, most of session rooms equipped with LCD Projector and a new cafeteria that you can enjoy. Check the photos here: <http://2011.barcampphnompenh.org/page/venue-and-date>

# Register yourself

To help the organizer, go to register yourself for the event: <http://2011.barcampphnompenh.org/node/add/attendee>

# Session Sharing

You need to post your expected talk topic as I stated above, participants need to vote the topics they like so that organizers could manage only the interesting topics but will see on barcamp website for their final decision.

Pre-posting the topic is good idea to attract more people to go to your presentation if yours does not got many votes, WHY NOT JUST CHANGE TO ANOTHER ONE?

Submit from here: <http://2011.barcampphnompenh.org/node/add/sessions>
