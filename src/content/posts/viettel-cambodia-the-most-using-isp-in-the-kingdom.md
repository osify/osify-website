Have a look to some of my sites on Google Analytic, **Viettel (Cambodia) Pte. LTD.** is the most uses service provider from users in Cambodia.

Beside Viettel, “**Cambodian ISP Country Wide Wireless IAP**” which is [CamGSM-Telesurf (MobiTel related)](http://www.telesurf.com.kh/) is the second one and **Latelz Co. Ltd** which is brought by [SmartMobile Cambodia](http://www.smart.com.kh/en/), the third.
