By inspiration of [Barcamp Phnom Penh](http://osify.com/labels/barcamp/), we had Barcamp Angkor last year and now it reaches the 2nd edition of the event in Seam Reap.

**BarCamp Angkor 2013** is a 2-day international technology conference and exhibition that brings together computer enthusiasts, young IT professionals, social innovators, web & mobile developers, bloggers, business owners, entrepreneurs, social media professionals and university students to learn & share about new forms of social innovation based on  technologies and tools that are bringing strong benefits to individuals and organizations.

[Join Free! Barcamp Angkor 2013!](http://angkor.barcampcambodia.org/)
