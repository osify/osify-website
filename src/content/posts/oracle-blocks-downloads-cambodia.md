Just recently, some people told me that we cannot download JDK or other products from Oracle website anymore. I have tested and found as they said, Cambodia is in Oracle’s blacklist:

> Thank you for accessing the Oracle Software Delivery Cloud. Due to your country location, we are unable to process your request. If you have an active support contract, you may request physical media by either submitting a [Service Request](https://support.oracle.com?ssSourceSiteId=splash) or calling [Customer Support](http://www.oracle.com/us/support/contact/index.html?ssSourceSiteId=splash). If you wish to purchase or evaluate our products on a 30-day trial please contact the appropriate [Sales Representative](http://www.oracle.com/us/corporate/contact/global-070511.html?ssSourceSiteId=splash) for your country.

That will be a big problem for us now.

I can proceed some download from [Java.net](http://Java.net) but any download from [Oracle.com](http://Oracle.com) is unable to do.
