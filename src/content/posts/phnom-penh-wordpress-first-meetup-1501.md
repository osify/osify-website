For your information of all wordpress guru, developers or even users. Some people is building a meetup of WordPress, to find out all experienced users & developers to share, to build up team or even to learn.

Myself also use WordPress, is happy to see WordPress Developers wave up in Cambodia!
First meetup:

> The first Phnom Penh WordPress meetup will be held Saturday January 11, 2011, from 12:30 to 1:30 p.m. at the new Hackerspace Phnom Penh building. For complete details, including agenda and location details, visit [here for detail](http://phnompenhmeetup.wordpress.com/2010/12/30/phnom-penh-wordpress-meetup-jan-15-2011/).
>
> **Place**: Hackerspace Phnom Penh
>
> **Date**: Saturday, 15th January 2011
> **Time**: 12:30 PM
> ([Meetup Detail Here](http://www.meetup.com/Phnom-Penh-Wordpress-Meetup/calendar/15859125/))
