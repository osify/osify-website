We haven’t spent any effort to help the team localized firefox to have Khmer but our thanks is to use it for our daily work to support our friends’ work, [Firefox Aurora Khmer](http://groups.google.com/group/mozilla.dev.l10n.km/browse_thread/thread/9c56108488421e0b?pli=1). And to promote more usage in Khmer Unicode!

We can now try Firefox in Khmer with [Firefox 12-beta](http://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/all-beta.html)
