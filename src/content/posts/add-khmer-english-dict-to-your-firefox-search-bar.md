Good online dictionary I like, I found Tovnah is a good one: <http://dictionary.tovnah.com>

You can add a search box feature to your firefox for this website, check it out:

- URL: <http://dictionary.tovnah.com/admin/>
- Go down and find: “Add **Khmer Online Dictionary** (KOD) to the Firefox search bar“
- Check screenshot:
