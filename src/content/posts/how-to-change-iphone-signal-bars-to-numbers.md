**Step 1:** Open phone pad and press: *3001#12345#* and then press “Call”

![](https://i1.wp.com/www.evernote.com/shard/s25/sh/fce10681-ded3-446e-ba4b-5915b1de238d/4d61d262d62b9fc856bf2bba7eae74f5/res/3dcd125b-d019-4e2b-a2da-f5ffed1ba430/IMG_0066.gif?resize=560%2C840&ssl=1 "Step 1")

*Step 1*

**Step 2:**  You will see this screen

![](https://i2.wp.com/www.evernote.com/shard/s25/sh/fce10681-ded3-446e-ba4b-5915b1de238d/4d61d262d62b9fc856bf2bba7eae74f5/res/a9fe59da-7647-4201-953d-fddb00059faf/IMG_0067.gif?resize=560%2C840&ssl=1 "Step 2")

*Step 2*

**Step 3:** Press and hold “Power” button until you see “Slide to Power Off” then release but don’t slide it.

**Step 4:** Press and hold “Home” button until it goes back to home screen then release.

That’s it.

*** If you want to get back to signal bar just follow step 1 and step 2 again then press “Home” button.
