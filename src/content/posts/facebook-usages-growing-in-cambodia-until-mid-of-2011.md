Do you know how facebook usage growth in Cambodia?

I found out a nice source on how growth of facebook usages in Cambodia that’s now, we have :

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total Facebook Users: | 308 420 | Penetration of population: | 2.09% |
| Position in the list: | 98. | Penetration of online pop.: | 395.41% |
| Average CPC: | $0.20 | Average CPM: | $0.09 |

The 308 420 users are not much if compare to other countries in the region, it might be because of most users are in Phnom Penh, the Internet service is not available every where or only in the town.

Checking via statistic by location, the most places usage are still in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap or some where in the towns only.
