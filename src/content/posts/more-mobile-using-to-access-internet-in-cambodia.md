According to some google analytic of mobile operation system, iOS & Android is the most use than other mobile operations now for Internet browsing. Both operating systems are keeping competing each other around the globe even Cambodia, a small country. Yesterday, [iOS](http://osify.com/labels/ios/#.UFs-XaCiFgo) announced iOS 6 and recently few months ago, [Android](http://osify.com/labels/android/#.UFs-IqCiFgo) announced its JB (Jelly Bean), version 4.1.
