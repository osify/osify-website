On Windows 7, in some case, when you click your Windows’ start menu, your search programs and file box is missing from there as this one:

[![windows7-search-box-missing](/media/20/209d03485956-windows7-search-box-missing.webp)](/media/20/209d03485956-windows7-search-box-missing.webp)

As we expected, the search box here as example:

[![windows7-search-box-expected](/media/43/4305466edba8-windows7-search-box-expected.webp)](/media/43/4305466edba8-windows7-search-box-expected.webp)

Here is the way to enable/disable the search box.

### Solutions

1. Open ***Control Panels***

2. Find ***Programs and Features***

3. In the left panel look for ***Turn Windows features On or Off***

![Step 2 & 3](/media/ee/ee1d62b108a9-windows7-control-panel-program-feature.webp)

*Step 2 & 3*

4. Scroll down the list and look for ***Windows Search*** and check the box

5. Click ***OK*** and then ***Yes*** on the Window

![Step 4 & 5](/media/dc/dc52371491c1-windows7-control-panel-program-feature-enable.webp)

*Step 4 & 5*

6. Restart to complete the change and you should find the Search on Start menu
