Are you using eclipse IDE?

Yes, I do. I started using Eclipse since 2006, the first IDE I started to stay with since then. As a developer, we are seeking for a good GUI or IDE, I found Eclipse as a central IDE for myself as I do not need to switch one for a programming language.

Now I use Eclipse for various purpose: JavaEE, PHP, Google App Engine, Android and with other framework integration.

As you are also using Eclipse (I hope most of Cambodians), you can submit your name as an individual user of eclipse from [here](http://www.eclipse.org/10birthday/submit/).

## Eclipse is now 10 years of their products’ presenting! Congratulation and keep up this great work!
