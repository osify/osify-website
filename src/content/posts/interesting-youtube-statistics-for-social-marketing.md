Do you know that there are **350 000 years** worth of video time are **watched every month on YouTube**?

[![TOP 15 countries with the most Total Uploaded Video Views by YouTube Channel](/media/de/de179ba09a8e-youtube-stat-shot-2012-05-29-at-09-27-40.webp "youtube-stat-shot-2012-05-29-at-09.27.40")](/media/de/de179ba09a8e-youtube-stat-shot-2012-05-29-at-09-27-40.webp)

TOP 15 countries with the most Total Uploaded Video Views by YouTube Channel

And do you know that…?

- The best brand channels on YouTube like Red Bull and Rovio Mobile have over 1700 years of video played, and 57 years every month.
- The best media channels on YouTube like IGN or Google have 11 000 years of video time played.
- The biggest brand channel on YouTube is Rovio Mobile (Angry Birds creator) with over 500 million video plays.

That’s from a [SocialBakers’](http://www.socialbakers.com/blog/590-most-interesting-youtube-statistics-hot/) data!

In Cambodia, many Karaoke songs/movies are uploaded to YouTube and some singers show there as well but on SocialBakers’ YouTube statistic page for Cambodia, we found only one person in the list, named [Kong](http://www.socialbakers.com/youtube-statistics/country/cambodia/page-1/).
